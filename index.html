Program profile

CCI AVTF pathway

Students move from technical foundations to commercial and broadcast production and then to student-led film and documentary work.

AVTF I

Technical Foundations of Production

  • Safety and professional ethics
  • Camera, audio, lighting, and editing fundamentals
  • Scriptwriting and production teams
  • Studio and field production

AVTF II

Commercial and Broadcast Production

  • Advanced preproduction and directing
  • Field-equipment workflows
  • Graphics and audio/video control systems
  • Commercials, news, and client projects

AVTF III

Short Film and Documentary Production

  • Student-led production teams
  • Short films, documentaries, and PSAs
  • Live-event and community coverage
  • Portfolio and career preparation
